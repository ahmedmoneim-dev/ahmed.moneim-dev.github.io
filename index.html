01AboutProfile · Background

Engineering across the full physical stack

“I care about systems that run reliably outside of a simulation window.”

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering graduate from Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA), who builds systems end to end — from SolidWorks design and firmware to sensor integration. I work across every layer of a physical system — from ladder logic and PLCs, to firmware on ESP32 and STM32, to deploying deep learning models on a Raspberry Pi with no GPU to spare.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of robotics, computer vision and industrial automation: autonomous navigation, object detection on edge hardware, and process control for production lines.

Most recently I worked as an Electrical & Instrumentation Engineer, testing and wiring industrial panels on-site — grounding the theory in how systems are actually built, inspected and maintained.
